The Her Lab

Using zebrafish to understand how genes shape metabolism and disease.

Explore our research

Research directions

Cancer in the liver and intestine

Zebrafish models help the lab investigate how changes in gene regulation contribute to cancers of the liver and intestine.

Lipids and metabolic disease

The group studies how disrupted lipid metabolism affects the liver and uses zebrafish to screen compounds that may modify these processes.

Genetic tools for zebrafish

The lab develops gene transfer and editing techniques to study gene function in the context of a living animal.

People

Principal investigator

Guor Mour Her, PhD

Professor Her studies molecular genetics, molecular medicine and developmental biology. He earned his bachelor’s and master’s degrees at National Taiwan Ocean University and his PhD at the University of Edinburgh in 1998. After postdoctoral research at the University of Pennsylvania and Academia Sinica, he joined the faculty of National Taiwan Ocean University in 2004 and National Yang-Ming University in 2018.
